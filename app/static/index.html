How it works

  • Drag across text to highlight it (yellow).
  • Shift + drag, or drag on a figure or blank area, to mark a region (orange box).
  • 📋 Copy, then drag across text, or Ctrl + drag at any time, to copy it, without making a highlight: it turns blue and goes to the clipboard as plain text, with no formatting. Paste it anywhere with Ctrl+V.
  • Prefer to type, or need to fix a word? Click the highlight and type in its box. Typed text is saved as it is.
  • Double-click a highlight and speak. Click Stop, press Ctrl+Enter, close the note, or double-click the next highlight to finish. The text fills in by itself later.
  • Click a highlight to read or type its comment. Esc closes it.
  • Drag the comment box by its title bar to see the text underneath, even while you speak (recording goes on). It stays where you leave it, also for the next comments; normal view and full screen each remember their own spot. Double-click the title bar to put it back under the highlight.
  • Ctrl+F (or 🔍) to search the text, also in full screen: Enter / Shift+Enter (or F3) go to the next / previous match, Esc or ✕ closes it. Matches are green; they never get in the way of highlighting.
  • ⛶ Full screen (or F): the page fills the whole screen height; Page Down / Page Up go page by page; Esc or F to leave.
  • Before you send the PDF, wait until nothing is queued (the window title shows "⏳ 3 left" until then). If you close the window earlier, a notification tells you when the file is ready to send. Until then, every unfinished comment says in the PDF itself that it is still being transcribed, so a file sent too early never hides a missing comment.
  • Recordings wait in a queue and are transcribed one at a time in the background, so you never wait: move on to the next comment. A blue badge means "queued"; the text fills in by itself. Closing the window, or even a crash or restart, does not lose a recording: it is finished and saved into the PDF later.
  • 🎤 on the top bar turns red while it listens. ⏳ 3 left means three recordings are still being transcribed.
  • ⚙ Settings: the languages you speak (any of 100), the microphone, dictate right after highlighting, safe mode and its rules, your initials, keeping your recordings, and where comments are saved.
  • 🛡 Safe mode (in ⚙): what you say is rewritten politely before it is written down (no swearing, no insults, no remarks about anyone's gender or origin; every technical point kept). Done on this computer.
  • Everything saves automatically (✓ on the top bar; point at it to see the file): a _review.pdf copy, so the original PDF is never changed, or, if you chose it in ⚙, the PDF itself (for Zotero).
  • Speech is transcribed on this computer. Nothing is sent to the cloud.
