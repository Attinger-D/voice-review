How it works

  • A badge over the video always says whether it is listening: red ● LISTENING, or grey NOT listening. ⛶ Full screen (F, or double-click the video) keeps it on screen.
  • Pause the video (Space, or the ⏸ button) and speak. The microphone is only on while the video is paused, so you never talk over it.
  • Play again (Space) to continue. Your comment is pinned to the moment you paused. You can refer to other moments by voice: "ten seconds ago…", "at one minute thirty…".
  • Pause and say nothing: no comment is kept.
  • Recordings are transcribed one at a time in the background; the text fills in by itself (blue = queued). Closing the window does not lose anything.
  • Click a comment, in the list or on the bar under the video, to jump there and read or edit it. ← → skip 5 seconds.
  • Speed: − and + on the top bar, also in full screen (top left), or the keys [ slower, ] faster, 5 % a step; double-click the number for normal speed. The voice keeps its pitch. Remembered for the next video.
  • The comment you are on is one line under the video (beside it, for a tall video): click it to read or type more.
  • 🎤 on the top bar turns red while it listens. ⚙ also holds the languages you speak (any of 100), the microphone and 🛡 safe mode.
  • ✕ Close file: closes this video (a recording in progress is kept) and lets you open another file in the same window.
  • Comments save automatically to <video>_review.md next to the video, plain text you can read or send. Speech never leaves this computer.
